The Paris week is unchanged. Then five extra days: a night facing Mont-Saint-Michel, a night in Bayeux by the D-Day coast, and three nights in London with the birthday dinner on Mon Dec 7. Same rhythm: slow mornings, one light anchor a day, one proper dinner.

Weather: Normandy and London in December are cool, grey and damp, highs around 45–48 °F, windy on the coast; sunset ~4:55 PM in Normandy and ~3:50 PM in London. Pack layers, a waterproof jacket and good shoes.

Flights (booked, United Polaris via Chicago): Out Sat Nov 28: UA5867 Traverse City 2:18 PM → O'Hare 2:55 PM, then UA987 O'Hare 6:45 PM → Paris CDG 9:55 AM Nov 29. Home Thu Dec 10 (open-jaw from London Heathrow T2): UA928 1:35 PM → O'Hare 4:55 PM (seats 4D/5F), then UA2304 O'Hare 7:45 PM → Traverse City 9:59 PM (First, 2E/2F).

Day 1

Sun Nov 29 · Arrive and settle

Anchor: None. Land, rest, tub.

MorningLand at CDG Terminal 1 mid-morning. Pre-booked car to the hotel (hotel car or Welcome Pickups). Drop bags at Hôtel Le Cinq Codet around noon.
AfternoonLatte at Noir – Rue de Grenelle, pastry from La Parisienne. Check in at 3 PM, nap and the tub. Optional wander down Rue Cler.
EveningDusk walk to the Champ de Mars (sunset ~4:57 PM; the Tower sparkles on the hour). 7:30 PM dinner at Bistrot des Fables. Jenny: salmon with sorrel or sea bream. Dessert: bourbon-vanilla crème brûlée.
Day 2

Mon Nov 30 · Montmartre at sunset + Moulin Rouge

Anchor: One evening outing.

MorningLate start. Latte at Dialogues Coffee, then tub and rest.
Afternoon1 PM lunch at Les Cocottes (Jenny: roast cod). ~3:15 taxi to Montmartre; dress smart for tonight. Ride the funicular up to Sacré-Cœur.
EveningSunset from the basilica steps (~4:56 PM), stroll Place du Tertre. Moulin Rouge Dinner & Show: doors 6:45, dinner with the orchestra at 7 PM, Féerie show at 9 PM. Jenny: the Belle Époque menu (smoked salmon, then pike-perch) — flag the shellfish allergy when booking. Smart dress, no trainers. Taxi home ~11 PM. Book only via reservations.moulinrouge.fr.
Day 3

Tue Dec 1 · Catacombs + lunch at Baguette

Anchor: The Catacombs, then a long lunch.

Morning~9:15 filter coffee at Coutume. Metro line 6 to Denfert-Rochereau. 10:30 timed entry at the Catacombes de Paris (~1 hr, 131 steps down / 112 up, ~14 °C inside — bring a layer, no big bags).
AfternoonTaxi to Montorgueil. 12:15 lunch at Baguette, French-Asian small plates. Jenny: salmon tartare with ginger, truffle risotto, smoked aubergine — skip the shrimp spring roll. Dessert: black-sesame crème brûlée. Keep it light. Taxi back, rest and tub.
Evening7:30 PM dinner at Café Caillou. Jenny: fresh salmon, mushroom risotto or aubergine gratin. Clark: a Chinon by the bottle.
Day 4

Wed Dec 2 · Versailles + Gordon Ramsay au Trianon

Anchor: The palace, then the starred dinner. Long day; taxi home.

Morning~9:15 pastry; dress smart casual for dinner. ~9:45 RER C from Champ de Mars–Tour Eiffel to Versailles Château Rive Gauche (~35–40 min). 11:00 timed entry, Palace of Versailles (Passport ticket).
Afternoon~1 PM lunch at Ore – Ducasse in the palace grounds. Jenny: truite aux amandes; skip the raw scallops. ~2:30 gardens, then the Trianons and Marie-Antoinette's Hamlet (last entry 4:45).
Evening~5 PM drinks at the Waldorf Astoria Trianon Palace bar: an Old Fashioned for Clark, Crémant for Jenny. 7:30 PM Gordon Ramsay au Trianon ★ — request a pescatarian, shellfish-free menu at booking (signatures use lobster and cockles). ~10:15 pre-booked taxi back to the hotel: the RER C closes evenings from 11 PM for works.
Day 5

Thu Dec 3 · The big-view day

Anchor: The 5 PM Seine cruise.

Morning~10:00 slow coffee at Dialogues. Optional walk over Pont d'Iéna to the Trocadéro esplanade for the classic Tower view.
Afternoon1 PM light lunch at Les Cocottes. Tub and rest. Leave 4:35, dressed warm.
Evening5 PM cruise with Bateaux Parisiens (Port de la Bourdonnais), back ~6 PM as the Tower sparkles. Old Fashioned at Minuit Express. ~7:45 dinner at Le Bosquet (Jenny: grilled cod, sauce vierge — oysters on the menu, flag the allergy).
Day 6

AfternoonLight lunch at Les Cocottes. Hotel spa and a long soak. Pack; confirm the morning car.
Evening7 PM farewell dinner at La Fontaine de Mars. Jenny: salmon in salt crust or roasted sea bass. Dessert: crème brûlée à la vanille. Lights out ~10.
Day 7

Ready to book

Sat Dec 5 · Paris → Mont-Saint-Michel

Anchor: Arrive at the Mont by sunset.

MorningSlow breakfast and check out of Le Cinq Codet (~10:30). Taxi to Paris-Montparnasse for a late-morning TGV to Rennes (~1 hr 30, book on SNCF Connect). Ready to book: trains open well ahead.
AfternoonPick up a premium sedan (the car makes Sunday's D-Day coast easy; the no-car fallback is the Keolis Rennes → Mont-Saint-Michel bus, daily and timed to the Paris TGVs) or SUV at SIXT Rennes station (one-way, returned in Caen on Monday). Easy, toll-free drive to the Mont (~1 hr 15). Check in at Le Relais Saint-Michel — ask for a room facing the Mont.
EveningSunset (~4:55 PM) from the hotel terrace, then the free shuttle across and a quiet walk up the lit, empty village. Dinner at the Relais Saint-Michel restaurant facing the lit Mont (open nightly 7–9:30 PM). Jenny: local fish of the day; Normandy is shellfish country, so flag the allergy every time.

Sun Dec 6 · The abbey + the D-Day coast

Anchor: Late-morning abbey, afternoon at the American Cemetery.

MorningBreakfast with the view. ~10:30 Mont-Saint-Michel Abbey (~1 hr 30, many steps; dress warm, the wind is sharp).
AfternoonLight lunch, then drive to the D-Day coast (~1 hr 45 on the A84). Normandy American Cemetery above Omaha Beach; arrive by ~2:30 to have daylight, the site closes at 5 PM.
Evening~20 min to Bayeux. Check in at Château de Bellefontaine. Walk to the lit cathedral. Dinner at Le Pommier (open Sunday evenings in winter, 6:30–8:30 PM; Normandy produce with real vegetarian and fish choices). La Rapière is closed on Sundays. Early-ish night.

Dining notes

Jenny: pescatarian, shellfish allergy

Je suis allergique aux fruits de mer et crustacés. Je mange du poisson mais pas de fruits de mer.
In London: "Pescatarian, with a shellfish allergy — no prawns, crab, lobster, scallops, mussels or oysters, and no shellfish stock."

Normandy menus lean on oysters, mussels and scallops; ask for the fish of the day (sole, bar, cabillaud, saumon). Jenny: Crémant or Champagne in France, English sparkling in London. Clark: bold reds, an Old Fashioned in the hotel bar, crème brûlée when it appears.
