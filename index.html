The Paris week is unchanged. Then five extra days: a night facing Mont-Saint-Michel (TGV to Rennes and a short drive), back to Paris for one night, the Eurostar on the birthday morning, and three nights in London with the birthday dinner on Mon Dec 7. Same rhythm: slow mornings, one light anchor a day, one proper dinner.

Weather: Mont-Saint-Michel and London in December are cool, grey and damp, highs around 45–48 °F, windy on the bay; sunset ~5 PM at the Mont and ~3:50 PM in London. Pack layers, a waterproof jacket and good shoes.

Risks: check SNCF strike notices about 48 hours before the Dec 5 and Dec 6 trains (and Eurostar for Dec 7). December daylight ends ~5 PM, so plan the drives in daylight.

Booked Flights (United Polaris via Chicago): Out Sat Nov 28: UA5867 Traverse City 2:18 PM → O'Hare 2:55 PM, then UA987 O'Hare 6:45 PM → Paris CDG 9:55 AM Nov 29. Home Thu Dec 10 (open-jaw from London Heathrow T2): UA928 1:35 PM → O'Hare 4:55 PM (seats 4D/5F), then UA2304 O'Hare 7:45 PM → Traverse City 9:59 PM (First, 2E/2F).

Morning

Afternoon

Evening
Open		7:30 PM dinner at Café Caillou. Jenny: fresh salmon, mushroom risotto or aubergine gratin. Clark: a Chinon by the bottle.

Sat Dec 5 · Paris → Mont-Saint-Michel

Anchor: TGV to Rennes, a short drive, and sunset over the Mount.

Morning
BookedLe Cinq Codet + TGV booked; taxi open		Slow breakfast and check out of Le Cinq Codet at 11:00. Taxi to Paris-Montparnasse. TGV INOUI 12219 Paris-Montparnasse 12:57 PM → Rennes 2:25 PM, 1st class, seats together (booked; e-tickets are in the SNCF Connect app, "Tickets" section).
Afternoon
BookedRelais + car booked		Lunch at Rennes station, then pick up the car at Sixt Rennes Gare at 2:30 PM (booked: VW T-Roc automatic or similar, flexible rate, pay at pickup; desk in the station hall, level 0, in front of McDonald's; Saturday hours 9–1 and 2–5). Bring license, passport and a credit card in Clark's name. Easy, toll-free drive (~1 hr 10, ~70 km on the A84 then N175) to the Mont. Check-in from 4 PM at Le Relais Saint-Michel (booked: Signature room with private terrace and view of the Mount, breakfast included, flexible rate with free cancellation before Nov 25). Before arriving, call the hotel for the access code to the hotel access road and parking: it is the last hotel before the bridge to the Mount, and reception is open 24 hours. Parking is paid at the parking machine on departure.
Evening
Open		Sunset (~5 PM) over the bay from the hotel, then the free shuttle across for a quiet walk up the lit, empty village. Dinner at the hotel restaurant facing the Mount, or in the village. Jenny: local fish of the day; this is shellfish country, so flag the allergy every time.

Dining notes

Jenny: pescatarian, shellfish allergy

Je suis allergique aux fruits de mer et crustacés. Je mange du poisson mais pas de fruits de mer.
In London: "Pescatarian, with a shellfish allergy — no prawns, crab, lobster, scallops, mussels or oysters, and no shellfish stock."

Menus around the Mont lean on oysters, mussels and scallops; ask for the fish of the day (sole, bar, cabillaud, saumon). Jenny: Crémant or Champagne in France, English sparkling in London. Clark: bold reds, an Old Fashioned at the hotel bar, crème brûlée when it appears.
